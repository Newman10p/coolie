===== Sector 5 =====
SECTORS 5 AND 6 Sector 5, the  Evolver , expands Coolie’s capabilities when you introduce a new business domain. Sector 6, the  Report Collector , gathers current information from the entire system and presents a coherent operational picture to the Orchestrator, which then presents it to you through the UI. They should be designed together because the Evolver creates new system components, while the Report Collector must automatically discover and report on those components. SECTOR 5: EVOLVER 1. Mission The Evolver is Coolie’s  controlled capability-expansion sector . It receives a request such as: &quot;Add the ability to operate an online tutoring business.&quot; It then determines: What Coolie already supports. What capabilities are missing. Whether the business is financially worth supporting. Which new agents, tools, workflows, connectors, policies, and data models are required. How much development and operating cost will be incurred. How the extension should be tested and integrated. Whether the new capability should be temporary, business-specific, or reusable across future businesses. The Evolver does not directly rewrite the live system. It creates and validates a  capability extension  and submits it for approval. 2.
